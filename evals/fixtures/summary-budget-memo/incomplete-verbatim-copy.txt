The facilities team reviewed three vendors for the office cleaning contract. Vendor A quoted $2,400 per month, Vendor B quoted $2,150 per month, and Vendor C quoted $2,900 per month but includes window cleaning and carpet care. After a two-week trial, the team found Vendor B missed several scheduled visits, while Vendor A completed every visit on time. The finance lead asked that the decision be made within 14 days so the new contract can start at the beginning of next quarter. The team recommends Vendor A because its price is close to the lowest quote and its reliability record is better.
