Stellar reaches agreement with the Stellar Consensus Protocol, in which validators vote in federated groups called quorum slices instead of mining blocks. A new ledger closes roughly every 5 seconds, and each ledger bundles the transactions agreed in that round. Once a ledger closes, its transactions are final, so there is no waiting for extra confirmations as on chains that can reorganize. TODO fill in the fee details here.
