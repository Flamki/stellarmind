Tomatoes grow best in full sun and well-drained soil that has been enriched with compost before planting. Regular, even watering keeps the fruit from cracking and helps the plants set more flowers. Staking or caging the plants improves air flow, which reduces the risk of leaf disease. Pinching off the lowest leaves stops soil splashing onto the foliage during heavy rain. Harvest the fruit when it is evenly colored and slightly soft to the touch.
